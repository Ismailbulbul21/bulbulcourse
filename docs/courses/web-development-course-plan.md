# Barashada Web Development with AI: course plan

**Title:** Barashada Web Development with AI
**Price:** $40
**Category:** Web Development
**Length:** 20 lessons in 8 modules, 1 hour 25 minutes
**Status:** hidden draft. Early access opens once Modules 1–2 are uploaded.
**Stack:** Claude Code, React + Vite, Supabase, WaafiPay, GitHub, Vercel

## Course description (on the site)

Koorsadan waxaad ku dhisaysaa dukaan online oo dhab ah adigoo adeegsanaya AI, xataa haddii aadan weligaa code qorin.

Dhammaadka waxaad haysataa website internetka ku jira: alaab, dambiil (cart), login, admin panel, iyo lacag bixin EVC, ZAAD iyo Sahal.

20 cashar oo kooban, cashar kastana wuxuu ku tusayaa wax cusub oo aad isla markiiba dhisayso.

Early access: casharro cusub ayaa la soo gelinayaa toddobaad kasta.

Ogow: Claude Code wuxuu u baahan yahay subscription gooni ah oo aad adigu iska bixinayso. Koorsada ayaa ku tusaysa sida loo iibsado.

## Recording schedule

Based on about 2.5 hours of work per finished hour of video.

| Week | Record | Finished video | Your work |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Modules 1–2, then early access opens | 20 min | About 1 h |
| 2 | Modules 3–4 | 23 min | About 1 h |
| 3 | Modules 5–6 | 22 min | About 1 h |
| 4 | Modules 7–8 | 20 min | About 1 h |

**Lesson 1.1 during early access:** your shop won't be finished yet, so show your live apps and explain the plan. Replace the video with a real shop tour after Module 8.

**Video export:** 720p MP4, and under about 8 MB per minute if you can. This costs students much less mobile data than your current videos.

## Lessons

**Length** is your target. **Show** is for you only and isn't on the site. **Description** is what students see.

### Module 1: Bilow (7 min)

| # | Lesson | Length | Show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Waxa aan dhisayno: dukaan online oo dhab ah (free preview) | 3 min | Finished shop on a phone: pick a product, pay, order arrives. |
| 1.2 | Vibe coding waa maxay? (free preview) | 4 min | Build something tiny live, then show AI making a mistake and you catching it. Good TikTok clip. |

- **1.1 description:** Waxaad arkaysaa dukaanka aad dhisi doonto, laga bilaabo alaabta ilaa lacag bixinta.
- **1.2 description:** Waxaad fahmaysaa sida AI kuu dhisto website, iyo waxa aanu kuu qaban karin. Hawsha: Dooro ganacsi aad dukaan u dhisayso, qorna 5 alaab oo uu iibiyo.

### Module 2: Diyaarinta Qalabka (13 min)

| # | Lesson | Length | Show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Ku rakib Node.js, VS Code iyo Git | 4 min | Install all three and check that each works. Create a GitHub account. |
| 2.2 | Iibso oo ku rakib Claude Code | 5 min | Say the real price. Buy it with a card you've tested, install it and log in. |
| 2.3 | Bogga kuugu horreeya adigoo isticmaalaya AI | 4 min | One sentence becomes a working page in the browser. Good TikTok clip. |

- **2.1 description:** Waxaad rakibaysaa qalabka aad u baahan tahay si aad u bilowdo.
- **2.2 description:** Waxaad iibsanaysaa Claude Code, waad rakibaysaa, waxaadna gelaysaa akoonkaaga.
- **2.3 description:** Waxaad dhisaysaa boggaagii ugu horreeyay. Hawsha: Bog ha kaaga shaqeeyo browser-ka, kuna kaydi GitHub.

### Module 3: Sida Loola Hadlo Claude (9 min)

| # | Lesson | Length | Show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Prompt wanaagsan iyo mid xun | 4 min | The same request written both ways, results side by side. Good TikTok clip. |
| 3.2 | Marka error la helo ama Claude qaldamo | 5 min | Fix a real error with Claude, then break something and undo it with Git. |

- **3.1 description:** Waxaad baranaysaa sida loo qoro prompt fiican oo natiijo wanaagsan keena.
- **3.2 description:** Waxaad baranaysaa sida error loo xalliyo iyo sida dib loogu noqdo. Hawsha: Hal error ku xalli Claude, Git-na dib ugu noqo.

### Module 4: Bogagga Dukaanka (14 min)

| # | Lesson | Length | Show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Bogga hore iyo naqshadda | 5 min | Design with AI so it doesn't look generic. Test on a real phone. |
| 4.2 | Bogga alaabta iyo hal alaab | 5 min | Product grid and details page, using sample products. |
| 4.3 | Dambiisha (cart) | 4 min | Add, remove, total, and keep the cart after refreshing. |

- **4.1 description:** Waxaad dhisaysaa bogga hore oo taleefanka ku fiican.
- **4.2 description:** Waxaad dhisaysaa bogga alaabta iyo bogga faahfaahinta alaabta.
- **4.3 description:** Waxaad dhisaysaa dambiisha. Hawsha: Alaab ku dar dambiisha, oo hubi inay hartay marka aad bogga cusboonaysiiso.

### Module 5: Database iyo Login (17 min)

| # | Lesson | Length | Show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | Samee Supabase iyo tables-ka | 5 min | Create a project and tables for products and orders. Explain which keys are secret. |
| 5.2 | Ku xir dukaanka database-ka | 4 min | Swap sample products for real ones, and upload product images. |
| 5.3 | Is-diiwaangelin iyo login | 4 min | Signup, login and logout. |
| 5.4 | Amniga: RLS | 4 min | Show one customer seeing another's orders, then fix it. Good TikTok clip. |

- **5.1 description:** Waxaad samaynaysaa database-ka dukaanka.
- **5.2 description:** Alaabtu waxay hadda ka imanaysaa database-ka, sawirradana waad soo gelinaysaa.
- **5.3 description:** Macaamiishu waxay samaysan karaan akoon, way geli karaan, wayna ka bixi karaan.
- **5.4 description:** Waxaad ilaalinaysaa xogta macaamiisha. Hawsha: Laba akoon ku tijaabi, hubina in midna aanu arki karin dalabka kan kale.

### Module 6: Admin Panel (5 min)

| # | Lesson | Length | Show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | Admin panel: alaabta iyo dalabaadka | 5 min | Admin-only access, add and edit products, see orders. Prove a normal account can't get in. |

- **6.1 description:** Milkiilaha dukaanku wuxuu maareyn karaa alaabta iyo dalabaadka. Hawsha: Alaab cusub ku dar adigoon code taaban.

### Module 7: Lacag Bixinta (12 min)

| # | Lesson | Length | Show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7.1 | WaafiPay iyo furayaasha sirta ah | 5 min | How mobile payment works, getting a merchant account, keeping keys in an Edge Function. |

| 7.3 | Haddii aadan haysan akoon: dalab WhatsApp | 3 min | An order button that sends the cart to WhatsApp. |

- **7.1 description:** Waxaad fahmaysaa sida lacagta mobilada u shaqeyso iyo sida furayaasha sirta ah loo ilaaliyo.
- **7.2 description:** Waxaad dhisaysaa bogga bixinta, oo lacag dhab ah ayaad ku tijaabinaysaa.
- **7.3 description:** Haddii aadan haysan akoon ganacsi, macaamiishu waxay kuugu soo dalban karaan WhatsApp. Hawsha: Hal dalab ka dhammee bilow ilaa dhammaad.

### Module 8: Internetka Geli (8 min)

| # | Lesson | Length | Show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8.1 | Vercel iyo domain | 5 min | Deploy, open it on a friend's phone, and connect a domain. Good TikTok clip. |

- **8.1 description:** Dukaankaagu hadda wuxuu ku jiraa internetka, oo magac u gaar ah ayuu leeyahay.
- **8.2 description:** Waxaad tijaabinaysaa dukaanka oo dhan, waxaadna baranaysaa sida macaamiil loo helo. Hawsha: Saaxiib ha kaa iibsado, link-gana la wadaag ardayda.
